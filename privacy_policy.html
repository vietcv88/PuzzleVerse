Privacy Policy for PuzzleVerse

Last updated: 11/03/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PuzzleVerse ("we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by CoderViet, collects, uses, and shares information from users ("you") of our mobile application available on Google Play and other platforms.

1. Application & Developer Information

App Name: PuzzleVerse

Developer (Publisher): CoderViet

Contact Email: vietcv88@gmail.com

PuzzleVerse is a mobile puzzle game that provides logic-based challenges, leaderboards, and optional in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

PuzzleVerse allows users to play without creating a traditional account.

If you choose to sign in or register (for example using email authentication), we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User ID generated by the authentication service

This information is used only for authentication, saving progress, and restoring purchases.

Device Information

We may collect limited device information automatically, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

Gameplay Data

PuzzleVerse may store gameplay-related information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Scores and achievements
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • In-game currency (coins, diamonds, hints)

In-App Purchase Data

If you make purchases inside the app:

  • Transactions are processed through Google Play Billing.
  • We receive a purchase verification token to confirm the transaction.

We do not store payment credentials, credit card numbers, or billing information.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services such as Google Analytics for Firebase to understand usage patterns. The collected data is aggregated and anonymous.

3. How We Use Your Information

App Functionality

To provide the core gameplay features of PuzzleVerse, including saving game progress, leaderboards, achievements, and restoring purchases.

Improvements & Debugging

Device information, usage statistics, and crash reports help us fix bugs, improve performance, and enhance gameplay experience.

Purchases

We verify purchase receipts with platform APIs to grant purchased items such as coins, diamonds, hint packs, or premium features.

Advertising

PuzzleVerse may display ads to support free gameplay. Advertising partners may use device identifiers to provide relevant ads.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or trade personal data with third parties.

Google Play Billing

Processes in-app purchases through Google Play.

Firebase Analytics

Collects anonymous usage metrics to help us understand engagement.
